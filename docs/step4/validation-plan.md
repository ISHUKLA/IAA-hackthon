# Step 4 validation preflight

Step 4 is a calendar-validation framework, not model training. F2022 trains 2021/validates 2022; F2023 trains 2021–22/validates 2023 and is primary; F2024 trains 2021–23/validates 2024 and is a maturity-sensitive challenge. All monetary labels have 31 December 2024 valuation and the mode is `retrospective_snapshot_proxy`, not a historical forecast.

The selected provisional input universe is the Step 2 repaired policy/claim candidate and its repaired observed baseline. It uses the labelled `exposure_clean` field once. The earlier full Step 3 bundle uses raw claims and is incompatible: its scenarios, reporting fits and target candidates are blocked from this contract rather than combined. Financial basis, deduplication, exposure and sum-insured adoption, monetary IBNR, future recovery and historical financial availability remain pending. Pooled unweighted power-1.5 Tweedie D² is a local provisional score only.
