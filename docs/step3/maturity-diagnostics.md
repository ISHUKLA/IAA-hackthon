# Maturity diagnostics and identification register

## Measured at the single snapshot

Among exactly matched claims, reporting delay ranges up to 1,361 days. There are 20,918 delays over 30 days, 5,745 over 90 days and 1,238 over one year. No negative delay or post-cutoff report was accepted. Count-only accident/report-month tables and summaries by line, peril, loss year, claim-age band and status are stored in the ignored maturity artifact. Monetary response exclusions are kept separate from this count population.

Motor bodily injury is the clearest long-tail priority: 7,554 claims, mean delay 157.8 days, 95th percentile delay 465 days, 38.10% open by claim count and 40.07% open by incurred. It carries 51,213,000.25 CU case reserve. Buildings subsidence has 1,291 claims, mean delay 114.9 days, 30.21% open by count, 32.39% open by incurred and 7,762,209.64 CU case reserve. The largest claim is open bodily injury with 11,894,276.93 CU incurred and 11,155,507.46 CU case reserve; it is preserved, not capped or deleted.

Own-damage and theft recovery patterns are described separately from case development. Observed recoveries are concentrated in motor and current open-claim zeros are not treated as final recovery forecasts. Delay/severity and delay/recovery correlations in the artifact are descriptive, selected associations only.

## Why recent cohorts are incomplete

At the valuation date, a claim is observed only when reporting delay does not exceed the time since loss. Longer delays are therefore absent from recent cohorts. An empirical CDF of observed delays is selected by this right truncation and is not an unbiased completeness factor. Recent lower mean severity and high open shares cannot identify a case-reserve multiplier.
