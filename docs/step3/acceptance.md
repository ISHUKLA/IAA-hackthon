# Step 3 acceptance and handoff

## Acceptance boundary

Step 3 is accepted by machine/AI review as a reproducible implementation, not as human actuarial approval. The deterministic linkage and observed response reconcile. The delay estimator is tested and backtested as a count diagnostic. The scenario engine passes accounting identities. None of those facts proves that the provisional financial basis or a case multiplier is financially correct.

`make step3-check` is a raw-data-free gate for linkage, accounting, aggregation, truncated likelihood, cutoffs, scenarios and failure cases. `make build-targets` atomically publishes ignored local outputs with raw, Step 2, config and uncommitted code provenance. `make step3-acceptance` rehashes every bundle file, checks current raw/Step 2 identity, row/count/money conservation, known audit references and Git exclusions. Step 1 and Step 2 retain their separate gates.

## Challenge conclusions

- Count completeness is not amount completeness; no monetary IBNR proxy was forced.
- Geometric tails and uniform daily occurrence are model assumptions; BI and subsidence remain the main instability priorities.
- Closed severity/recovery evidence is selected and cannot identify open-claim development.
- Reserve release is permitted; strengthening is not forced by open status.
- The 11.89m CU BI claim is preserved and its scenario contribution is explicit.
- There are no fabricated historical financial snapshots, hidden-claim allocations, validation splits, pricing models, test targets or predictions.

## Handoff

Step 4 may use the immutable manifest, deterministic observed response, separate scenario components, count-completeness/effective-exposure diagnostics and cutoff-explicit fit/transform interface. It must refit reporting models within permitted folds and keep the pending A03 financial basis visible. Human decisions on financial basis, repudiated/declined terminology, ACT/365 adoption, sum-insured adoption and final target selection remain pending.
